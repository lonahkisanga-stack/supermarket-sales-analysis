# Supermarket Sales Analysis (Excel)

## Project Overview
This project analyzes supermarket sales data to find out which product lines, cities, customer types, payment methods and months generate the most sales. The analysis was done in **Microsoft Excel** using data cleaning, PivotTables and PivotCharts, followed by business insights and recommendations.

**Total sales analyzed: 322,966.75**

## Why This Project
Before moving into data analysis, I worked as a **Direct Sales Representative at SBM Bank Kenya**, selling banking products directly to customers. That experience taught me that good sales decisions come from understanding who buys, what they buy, how they pay, and when and where sales happen. This project applies that same thinking using data.

## Dataset
- **Source:** [Supermarket Sales – Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/aungpyaeap/supermarket-sales)
- **Period:** January – March 2019
- **Columns:** 17, including Invoice ID, Branch, City, Customer Type, Gender, Product Line, Unit Price, Quantity, Total, Date, Payment Method and Rating
- The raw data screenshot below shows a sample of the dataset. The full dataset is available on Kaggle at the link above.

## Data Cleaning
1. Downloaded the supermarket sales dataset from Kaggle and opened it in Excel.
2. Created a copy of the original data and named the sheets **Raw Data** and **Cleaned Data**, keeping the original data unchanged.
3. Inspected the dataset and its 17 columns.
4. Checked for duplicate records and found **zero duplicates**.
5. Checked for blank or missing entries.
6. Sorted and filtered the data to inspect it, including arranging dates from oldest to newest and checking the payment method entries.
7. Added a calculated total column to verify the sales figures.
8. Added a matching column to confirm that the calculated figures agreed with the original sales figures.
9. Kept the original 17 columns in the final cleaned dataset.

## Analysis Process
1. Created PivotTables to summarise the data.
2. Analysed sales by product line.
3. Analysed sales by city.
4. Compared Member and Normal customers.
5. Compared payment methods.
6. Analysed sales by month.
7. Created PivotCharts to present the results.
8. Developed five business questions, wrote three insights and one recommendation for each, and added an overall recommendation.

## Business Questions
1. Which product lines generate the highest sales?
2. Which city generates the highest sales?
3. Which customer type contributes more to sales?
4. Which payment method generates the highest sales?
5. During which months are sales highest?

## Insights and Recommendations

### 1. Which product lines generate the highest sales?
- Food and Beverages is the strongest-performing product line, generating the highest sales of 56,144.84.
- Health and Beauty is the weakest-performing product line, recording the lowest sales of 49,193.74. This may be an area that requires further attention.
- Sales across the six product lines are relatively balanced, although Food and Beverages leads and Health and Beauty trails.

**Recommendation:** Maintain sufficient stock of strong-selling products and review the pricing, variety and promotion of Health and Beauty products to improve sales.

### 2. Which city generates the highest sales?
- Naypyitaw recorded the highest sales at 110,568.71.
- Yangon followed closely with 106,200.37.
- Mandalay recorded the lowest sales at 106,197.67.

**Recommendation:** The supermarket should maintain its performance in Naypyitaw and investigate opportunities to increase sales in Yangon and Mandalay.

### 3. Which customer type contributes more to sales?
- Members generated the highest sales at 189,694.76.
- Normal customers generated sales of 133,271.99.
- Members contributed approximately 58.7% of total sales.

**Recommendation:** The supermarket should strengthen its membership programme and encourage normal customers to become members through attractive loyalty benefits.

### 4. Which payment method generates the highest sales?
- Cash generated the highest sales at 112,206.57.
- E-wallet followed with sales of 109,993.11.
- Credit card recorded the lowest sales at 100,767.07.

**Recommendation:** The supermarket should continue supporting all three payment methods while maintaining reliable cash and e-wallet services and exploring ways to encourage credit card usage.

### 5. During which months are sales highest?
- January recorded the highest sales at 116,291.87.
- March followed with sales of 109,455.51.
- February recorded the lowest sales at 97,219.37.

**Recommendation:** The supermarket should investigate what contributed to January's strong sales and use those findings to develop strategies for improving sales during slower months, particularly February.

## Overall Recommendation
Sales are fairly balanced across product lines, cities and payment methods, so the biggest opportunity lies with customers. The supermarket should grow its membership programme, since members bring in about 58.7% of sales. It should also boost Health and Beauty through better pricing, variety and promotion, use lessons from Naypyitaw and from January's strong sales to lift weaker branches and slower months like February, and keep all payment options reliable for customers.
